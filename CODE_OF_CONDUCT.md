# Code of conduct

This repository publishes the source of a commercial website and accepts **issue
reports** about it. That is the whole community here, and the standard is short.

## In issues

- Be civil. Critique the work, not the person.
- One issue per problem, with a URL or a file path.
- Accessibility and broken-link reports are always welcome, however small.
- No advertising, no link-dropping, no "I can build this for you" pitches.
- No disclosure of anyone's personal data, including your own.

## Not here

- **Work enquiries** belong on [Telegram](https://t.me/relaystack).
- **Security reports** must not be public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Pull requests** for the site's copy, design or pricing are not accepted. See
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Enforcement

I moderate this repository alone. Anything that breaks the two lines above gets
closed, and repeat behaviour gets blocked. There is no appeals committee — that is
what a one-person business looks like — so the standard is deliberately kept to
things most people already do.

## Attribution

Deliberately not the Contributor Covenant. That document is written for projects
with maintainers, contributors and a review process. Borrowing it here would be
costume.
